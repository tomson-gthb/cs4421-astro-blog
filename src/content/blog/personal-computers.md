Desktops and Laptops

Points:

    Intel Core i3/i5/i7

    AMD Ryzen

    Apple M1

Concepts:

    Desktop/laptop CPUs:

        General-purpose, high-performance processors.

        Run full OSes (Windows, macOS, Linux) and heavy applications (IDEs, games, video editing).

    Intel Core i3/i5/i7:

        x86-64 CPUs, multiple cores, hyperthreading, large caches.

        i3: entry-level; i5: mid-range; i7: high-end (in a given generation).

    AMD Ryzen:

        Competing x86-64 family, often strong multi-core performance.

        Similar tiers (Ryzen 3/5/7/9).

    Apple M1:

        ARM-based SoC designed by Apple for Macs.

        Integrates CPU, GPU, memory, etc. on one chip.

        High performance per watt; runs macOS on ARM.

These are the “classic” personal computers you likely use daily.
