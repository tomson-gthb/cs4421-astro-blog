Serengeti National Park is Africa's most celebrated wildlife sanctuary, located in northern Tanzania. It covers a massive 14,763 square kilometers of protected wilderness. The park is a UNESCO World Heritage Site famous for holding the highest density of large carnivores on the planet.

Key Features

• The Great Migration: An unaltered annual cycle where over 1.5 million wildebeest and hundreds of thousands of zebras trek across the plains.
• The Big Five: Pristine habitats support thriving populations of lions, leopards, elephants, buffaloes, and rare black rhinos.
• Distinct Sectors: Features short-grass savanna in the south, crocodile-infested rivers in the western corridor, and open woodlands in the north.
• Moru Kopjes: Iconic granite rock outcrops that break up the flat plains, frequently used by lions and cheetahs as lookout points.

Travel & Logistics

• Transit & Lodging: Accessible by driving from Arusha or catching bush flights to Seronera Airstrip (SEU). Accommodations range from basic public campsites to ultra-luxury tented camps
