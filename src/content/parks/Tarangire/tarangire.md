Tarangire National Park is a scenic wildlife sanctuary in northern Tanzania. It is part of the famous northern safari circuit but offers a quieter, less congested experience than the Serengeti. The park is famous for its massive elephant populations and vast landscapes dotted with ancient baobab trees.

## Key Features

* **The Tarangire River**: The primary lifeblood of the park. During the dry season, it draws huge, migrating herds of wildlife to its banks.
* **Elephant Paradise**: The park holds one of the highest concentrations of breeding elephants in East Africa, with herds numbering up to 300.
* **Prehistoric Skyline**: Giant, 1,000-year-old baobab trees tower over the savannah grasslands. They create a highly distinct, recognizable landscape.
* **Active Bush Safaris**: Unlike some neighboring parks, Tarangire permits thrilling guided walking safaris and nocturnal night game drives.

## Travel & Logistics

* **Access**: Located about 120 kilometers southwest of Arusha. It is easily reached via a comfortable, 2-to-3-hour drive on a paved highway.
