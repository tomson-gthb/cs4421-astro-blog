Kilimanjaro National Park is a world-famous protected area in northern Tanzania. It completely surrounds Mount Kilimanjaro, the highest mountain in Africa and the tallest free-standing peak on Earth (5,895 meters). The park is a designated UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Key Features

• Three Volcanic Cones: The massif includes Kibo (the dormant, snow-capped summit), Mawenzi (a rugged peak), and Shira (an ancient collapsed plateau).
• Five Ecological Zones: A single climb transitions from lush tropical rainforest up through moorland, alpine desert, and finally an arctic summit ice field.
• Glaciers: The high peaks host rapidly receding equatorial glaciers, making it a critical area for climate change research.

Trekking & Travel

• Official Routes: The mountain features several approved paths, including the popular Machame, Lemosho, and Marangu routes (the only route offering sleeping huts instead of tents).
• Regulations: All hikers must climb with registered guides and porters. Children under 10 years old are restricted from climbing above 3,700 meters.
