Mikumi National Park is Tanzania's fourth-largest wildlife sanctuary. It is located in south-central Tanzania near Morogoro. The park features open, golden horizons often compared to a "mini-Serengeti". It is highly popular due to its excellent wildlife density and easy accessibility.

Key Features

• Mkata Floodplain: The park's main scenic focus. This savanna grassland hosts the highest concentration of wildlife.
• The Big Four: Visitors can regularly spot elephants, lions, buffaloes, and leopards. Rhinos are not present.
• Tree-Climbing Lions: A unique local highlight. Lions often lounge on ancient termite mounds to view the plains.
• Rich Birdlife: Over 400 species thrive here. Notable residents include the colorful lilac-breasted roller.

Economy & Travel

• A7 Highway: The main tarmac road cuts directly through the park. This makes it the only major park practical for self-drive tours.
• Transit Options: It is a 4-to-5-hour drive from Dar es Salaam. Travelers can also take quick fly-in day trips from Zanzibar to the park's central airstrip.
