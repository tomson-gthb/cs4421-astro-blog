Saadani National Park is a unique wildlife sanctuary in eastern Tanzania. It covers 1,100 square kilometers along the Indian Ocean. It is the only national park in East Africa where the African bush directly meets a tropical beach.

Key Features

• Beachfront Safaris: Visitors can watch elephants, buffaloes, and giraffes walk right along the sandy shores.
• The Wami River: A freshwater river forming the southern park border. Boat safaris offer close-up views of large pods of hippos and massive Nile crocodiles.
• Madete Beach: A highly protected nesting ground located at the northern tip of the park. It is a critical breeding site for threatened green turtles.
• Mafui Sandbank: An offshore coral reef strip that appears at low tide. It offers great underwater visibility for snorkeling and diving.

Economy & Travel

• Zanzibar Link: The park's location makes it a popular quick escape for beach travelers. It is just a 15-minute flight from Zanzibar Island.
• Transit: It can be accessed via domestic charter flights to the Saadani airstrip or via a 4-to-5-hour drive from Dar es Salaam.
