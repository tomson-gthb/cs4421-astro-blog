Mafia Island is a peaceful, secluded tropical island in Tanzania. It sits in the Indian Ocean south of Zanzibar. Unlike its busy neighbors, it is an untouched paradise. It is famous for its quiet beaches and rich marine life.

## Key Attractions

* **Whale Sharks**: The biggest attraction on the island. Visitors can swim beside these gentle giants. The peak season runs from October to March.
* **Mafia Island Marine Park**: A vast protected ocean reserve. It features coral reef gardens and pristine diving sites.
* **Chole Island**: A tiny, historic island inside Chole Bay. It holds old Swahili ruins and a mangrove forest.
* **Juani Island**: A wild island nearby. It features the ancient Kua Ruins and a hidden coral grotto called the Blue Lagoon.

## Economy & Travel

* **Lifestyle**: The local economy revolves around fishing and small-scale farming.
* **Transport**: Travelers arrive via short flights to Mafia Airport from Dar es Salaam or Zanzibar. Most local transport uses motorbikes or small boats.
