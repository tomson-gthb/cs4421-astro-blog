Pemba Island is a lush, hilly island in Tanzania's Zanzibar Archipelago. It sits north of Unguja (Zanzibar Island) in the Indian Ocean. Locals call it "The Green Island" due to its rich soil and dense vegetation. It is famous for its massive clove plantations and world-class diving.

Key Attractions

• Misali Island: A protected marine sanctuary off the west coast. It features coral reefs, sea turtles, and pristine beaches.
• Ngezi Forest Reserve: A rare, protected rainforest at the northern tip. It is home to the endemic Pemba flying fox (a giant fruit bat).
• Chake Chake: The island's central and largest town. It sits on a high hill overlooking a mangrove-filled bay.
• Clove Plantations: Vast farms spread across the island. Pemba produces the majority of Zanzibar's world-famous clove exports.

Economy & Travel

• Diving: The island is bounded by deep ocean trenches. It offers dramatic vertical coral walls and exceptional underwater visibility.
• Transport: Travelers arrive via short flights to Pemba Airport from Zanzibar or Dar es Salaam, or via ferry.
