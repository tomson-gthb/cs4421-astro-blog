Tanga is a historic port city in northeastern Tanzania. It sits right on the Indian Ocean. It is the second largest seaport in the country. It is famous for its quiet, flat, and bicycle-friendly streets.

## Key Attractions

* **Amboni Caves**: Massive underground limestone caverns. They feature towering stalactites.
* **Tongoni Ruins**: A 14th-century Swahili settlement. It holds ancient mosques and tombs.
* **Toten Island**: An island in the harbor. It contains ruins and thick mangrove forests.
* **Urithi Museum**: A local history museum. It is housed in an old colonial building.

## Economy & Travel

* **Sisal Trade**: The city was once a global hub for making ropes from sisal plants.
* **Transport**: It has a small airport and a railway link. Most locals travel by bicycle.
