Arusha is the safari capital of northern Tanzania. It sits at the base of Mount Meru at a high elevation. The city has a cool, pleasant climate year-round. It is the main gateway for world-famous wildlife tours.

Key Attractions

• Mount Meru: A towering active volcano that dominates the skyline. It offers challenging multi-day mountain climbs.
• Arusha National Park: A nearby wildlife park. It features the Ngurdoto Crater, pristine lakes, and diverse bird species.
• Cultural Heritage Centre: A massive museum and art gallery. It showcases African carvings, gems, and tribal history.
• Clock Tower: The historic center point of the city. Locals consider it the exact midpoint between Cairo and Cape Town.

Economy & Travel

• Safari Gateway: The starting point for trips to Serengeti, Ngorongoro Crater, and Lake Manyara National Park.
• Transit Hub: The city is served by Arusha Airport for domestic flights and Kilimanjaro International Airport for global travel.
