Mbeya is a major city located in southwestern Tanzania. It sits in a high-altitude mountain valley. It serves as a vital trade hub near the Zambia and Malawi borders. The city is famous for its cool climate and lush green hills.

## Key Attractions

* **Lake Ngozi**: A breathtaking, emerald-green crater lake. It is reached by hiking through dense rainforests.
* **Kitulo National Park**: A unique botanical park. Locals call it the "Bustani ya Mungu" (God's Garden) due to its rare wildflowers.
* **Mount Rungwe**: A massive dormant volcano. It offers challenging treks and superb views.
* **Daraja la Mungu**: A striking, natural rock bridge formation spanning the Kiwira River.

## Economy & Travel

* **Agriculture**: The region is Tanzania's breadbasket. It produces vast quantities of coffee, tea, and bananas.
* **Transport**: It is linked by the TAZARA Railway and TAZARA Road. It has direct flights via Songwe Airport.
