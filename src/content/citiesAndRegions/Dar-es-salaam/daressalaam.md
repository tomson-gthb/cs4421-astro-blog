Dar es Salaam is the largest city and commercial powerhouse of Tanzania. It sits on a massive natural harbor along the Indian Ocean. While it is no longer the political capital, it remains the economic, fashion, and media heart of the country. It is one of East Africa's fastest-growing cities.

## Key Attractions

* **Kivukoni Fish Market**: A buzzing, high-energy waterfront market. It shows the raw, authentic pulse of the city's daily fishing trade.
* **National Museum & House of Culture**: A peaceful cultural hub. It houses critical human fossil discoveries and deep tribal history.
* **Coco Beach**: A lively public sandy beach on the Msasani Peninsula. It is packed with local street food vendors and weekend music.
* **Village Museum**: An open-air museum showcasing traditional Tanzanian tribal homes. It features live cultural dance performances.

## Economy & Travel

* **Global Port**: The city's massive seaport serves as the primary trade gateway for landlocked nations like Zambia, Malawi, and Rwanda.
* **Transit Hub**: It acts as the main gateway for tourists via Julius Nyerere International Airport. It connects travelers to Zanzibar ferries and mainland safaris.
