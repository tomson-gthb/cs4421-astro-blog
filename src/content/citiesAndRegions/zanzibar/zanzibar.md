Zanzibar is a tropical archipelago off the coast of Tanzania. Its main island is named Unguja. It sits in the Indian Ocean. The island blends African, Arabic, and Indian cultures.

## Key Areas

* **Stone Town**: The historic heart of the island. It features winding alleys, spice markets, and carved wooden doors.
* **Nungwi & Kendwa**: Vibrant northern villages. They feature white-sand beaches, clear turquoise water, and luxury resorts.
* **Paje**: A lively southeast beach town. It is a world-famous spot for kitesurfing.

## Top Activities

* **Spice Tours**: Guided walks through local farms. Visitors taste fresh cloves, nutmeg, and vanilla.
* **Prison Island**: A short boat ride from Stone Town. It is home to giant Aldabra tortoises.
* **Safari Blue**: A popular full-day sailing trip. It includes snorkeling, sandbanks, and seafood.
