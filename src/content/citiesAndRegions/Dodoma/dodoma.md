Dodoma is the official capital city of Tanzania. It is located in the center of the country. It replaced Dar es Salaam as the capital to centralize government services. It sits on a dry, sunny plateau.

Key Attractions

• The Bunge: The striking, circular Tanzanian Parliament building. It anchors the city's political life.
• Gaddafi Mosque: One of the largest mosques in East Africa. It features beautiful, modern Islamic architecture.
• Nyerere Square: A central plaza dedicated to Tanzania's first president. It is a popular local meeting spot.
• Lion Rock: A large rocky hill on the edge of town. It offers great panoramic views of the city.

Economy & Travel

• Wine Production: Dodoma is the heart of Tanzania's wine industry. It produces famous local dry red and white wines.
• Transport: It links major highways from east to west. It features a central railway station and Dodoma Airport.
