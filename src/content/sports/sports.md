## Culture & Banter

* **Utani wa Jadi**: A traditional custom of playful mocking used to defuse social tension. It governs the fierce but peaceful rivalry between Simba and Yanga.
* **Social Split**: The rivalry cleanly divides families, workplaces, and communities into two passionate halves.
* **Friendly Mocking**: Fans engage in constant media teasing and humorous accusations of witchcraft (ushirikina). Rivals are treated as friends, allowing banter even at solemn events like funerals.

## Patriotism & Unity

* **Taifa Stars**: The Tanzanian national team serves as an essential pillar of national pride and unity.
* **The Match Truce**: Whenever the national team plays international tournaments like AFCON, an absolute club truce is declared across the country.
* **National Pride**: Fans instantly trade their red or yellow club jerseys for the blue, yellow, and green colors of the national flag. This unites the nation under a shared Swahili identity (Utanzania).
